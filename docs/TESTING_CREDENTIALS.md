## 🚀 How to Access & Switch Dashboards

### Method 1: Instant Top Navbar Role Switcher
- In the top header bar of the screen, click the **"Switch Role"** dropdown button anytime to jump directly between **Student**, **Teacher**, **Principal**, **Finance Officer**, **Accountant**, **HR Manager**, **Parent**, and **Super Admin**.

### Method 2: Sidebar Profile Switcher
- In the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, click the **User Profile card** to switch between any stakeholder view in real-time.

### Method 3: Sign-In Authentication Flow
- Navigate to [`/login`](http://localhost:3000/login) and sign in using any of the official institutional school emails listed above.
